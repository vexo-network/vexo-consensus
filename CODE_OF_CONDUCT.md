# Code of Conduct

`vexo-consensus` welcomes contributors who want to build reliable, reviewable, and safer consensus infrastructure.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but ask for clarification when something is unclear.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence, tradeoffs, tests, and maintainability.
- Help new contributors understand the project boundaries and safety requirements.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Pressuring maintainers to weaken security, audit, or production-readiness requirements.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers or repository owners. Security vulnerabilities should be reported through [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), not through public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
